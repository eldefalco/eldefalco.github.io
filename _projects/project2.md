<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>
Infant-directed speech exhibits a markedly different acoustic and prosodic profile than adult-directed speech, yet speech emotion recognition systems are rarely trained or evaluated on infant-directed data. The lack of curated datasets limits the applicability of existing models to early caregiver–infant interactions.
</p>

<hr />

<h2>Approach</h2>
<p>
This project, conducted with Dr. Christina Zhao at the LEAP Lab at the Institute for Learning and Brain Sciences, University of Washington, constructs a <strong>novel emotional speech corpus</strong> from lab recordings of <strong>78 infant–parent dyads</strong> interacting in a controlled play environment.
</p>

<hr />

<h2>Takeaway</h2>
<p>
The project will investigate how emotion in infant-directed speech diverges acoustically from adult-directed norms and highlight the value of <strong>curated datasets and models in specialized speech genres</strong> for building emotion recognition systems.
</p>

<hr />

<h2>Methods</h2>
<ul>
  <li>
    Creation of a <strong>new emotional speech corpus</strong> from 78 infant–parent pairs recorded in a controlled laboratory play setting.
  </li>
  <li>
    Audio-only and audiovisual data form a multimodal corpus of spontaneous emotional caregiver speech.
  </li>
  <li>
    Supervised and Self-Supervised modeling capturing harmonic, inharmonic, prosodic, and temporal properties of infant-directed emotional speech.
  </li>
  <li>
    Integration of human perceptual ratings to evaluate emotional salience.
  </li>
  <li>
    Comparative analysis assessing how SER models trained on adult-directed speech generalize to infant-directed contexts.
  </li>
</ul>

<h2>Tools &amp; Techniques</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Signal Processing for infant-directed speech</li>
  <li>Audio recording and controlled lab data collection</li>
  <li>Corpus design and annotation for developmental speech</li>
  <li>Human perceptual data integration</li>
  <li>Emotion model evaluation and error analysis</li>
  <li>Data cleaning, normalization, and time-series management for large-scale recordings
</ul>
